# Electoral Threat and Digital Repression

This repository documents an ongoing research project on the relationship between electoral risk and digital repression.

## Project overview

The project examines how electoral pressures shape incumbents' use of digital repression and how these responses vary across political regimes.

The empirical analysis uses a cross-national country-year panel covering **2000–2020** and draws on established datasets on digital repression, elections, and political institutions.

## Methods

The project applies complementary panel-data approaches, including:

- **PanelMatch**
- **Two-way fixed effects (TWFE)**
- event-time and placebo diagnostics
- robustness and sensitivity analyses

These methods are used to evaluate changes in digital repression around periods of electoral risk and to examine heterogeneity across political regimes.
